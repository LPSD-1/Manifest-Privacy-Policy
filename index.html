Manifest — Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

This policy covers the Android applications Manifest and Manifest Studio (“the apps”), published by Lucas Potter (“we”, “us”).

The short version. We do not operate a server, we do not have accounts, and we never receive your data. Everything the apps record stays on your device unless you or your organisation configure the apps to send it somewhere — and then it goes to the destination you chose, not to us.

1. Who controls the data

The apps are supplied as tools. Where a business installs them and uses them to record work, that business is the data controller for anything recorded, and we are neither a controller nor a processor of it, because it never reaches us.

We are the controller only for information you send us directly, such as an email asking for support.

2. What the apps record on your device

A parts catalogue contains no personal data. Personal data can enter the apps through the Jobs feature, when the catalogue your organisation has authored includes fields of these kinds:

Field kindWhat it can hold
PersonA name — typically the engineer, or the customer signing off
SignatureA handwritten signature, captured on screen
PhotoPhotographs taken with the device camera
AudioVoice notes recorded through another app on the device
VideoVideo recorded through another app on the device
Address, phone, emailContact details typed into a job

The apps also store a personal note and a favourites list per part, which are held only on the device that typed them and are never included in a catalogue sent to anyone else.

3. Where that data goes

By default, nowhere. The apps are offline-first. A job stays on the device that recorded it.

A Manifest Studio operator may configure one or more destinations for completed jobs. If they do, the job — including any names, signatures, photographs, recordings and video it holds — is sent to that destination. The available transports are:

  • HTTPS — posted to a web address the operator specifies, over an encrypted connection
  • Email — handed to an email app on the device
  • File — written to a location on the device or to storage the operator chooses

We do not choose these destinations, do not see them, and receive nothing. Once a job reaches a destination, the privacy practices of whoever operates that destination apply to it.

A Manifest Studio operator may also run a catalogue server on their own network to distribute catalogues to their own devices. That server is theirs and runs on their hardware.

4. What we collect

Nothing. The apps contain no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no tracking of any kind. There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

Purchases in Manifest Studio are handled by Google Play. We receive from Play only the confirmation that a purchase exists. We never see your payment details. Google’s handling of that transaction is covered by Google’s own privacy policy.

The barcode scanner uses Google’s ML Kit barcode library, bundled inside the app. It runs on the device and no image leaves the device for scanning.

5. How data is protected on the device

  • The database is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher, with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Catalogues shared between devices are encrypted and are readable only by devices the organisation has paired.
  • Backups you create are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We cannot recover that passphrase, and neither can anyone else.
  • Data sent over HTTPS is encrypted in transit.

6. Permissions

PermissionWhy
CameraScanning codes, and taking photographs on a job. Optional — the apps work without it
InternetSending completed jobs to a destination your organisation configured, and fetching catalogue updates
VibrationConfirming a successful scan

The apps request no location, contacts, microphone or background permissions.

7. How long data is kept

Jobs stay on the device until deleted. A catalogue may specify a retention period, after which the apps automatically anonymise or delete completed jobs on the device. Deleted jobs go to a bin and are permanently erased after seven days.

You can delete any job at any time. Clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings removes everything the app holds on that device, permanently.

8. Children

The apps are business tools and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

9. Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, requests to access, correct, export or erase job data should go to the business that operates the app — they hold it. The apps let them export and delete it on the device.

If you have contacted us directly and want that correspondence deleted, ask and we will delete it.
